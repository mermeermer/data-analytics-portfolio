NETFLIX BUSINESS INSIGHTS REPORT
=================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The dataset contains 8,790 unique titles. Movies represent 69.69% and TV Shows represent 30.31% of the titles.
The most frequent recorded rating is TV-MA (3,205 titles).
The most frequent genre is International Movies (2,752 titles).

2. KEY FINDINGS
Content type: Movie is the largest content type in the dataset.
Country: United States ranks first by title-country associations (3,240).
Genre: International Movies ranks first by genre-title associations.
Release years: the earliest recorded year is 1925, and the latest recorded year is 2021.
The year with the largest number of titles in this dataset is 2018, with 1,146 titles.
Titles without country information: 287.

3. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
Catalog planning: review the current balance between Movies and TV Shows alongside business goals and customer demand.
Content localization: investigate country-level catalog coverage and regional viewing data before prioritizing new local productions or licensing.
Genre strategy: compare leading genres with viewing hours, completion rates, subscriber retention, and production costs before making investment decisions.
Audience suitability: use content ratings to improve catalog filtering and age-appropriate discovery, while following the applicable rating definitions.
Data quality: maintain consistent country, rating, and genre values and monitor records without country information.

4. IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
This dataset describes titles and their metadata, not actual views, revenue, profit, subscriber preferences, or engagement.
Country counts are title-country associations. A title associated with multiple countries contributes to each one.
Genre counts are title-genre associations. A title may belong to multiple genres.
The latest release year in this dataset is not necessarily the current year. Do not interpret the dataset as a complete or live Netflix catalog.
Business recommendations are hypotheses to investigate. They should be validated using viewing, revenue, cost, and subscriber data before operational decisions are made.
