DATASET OVERVIEW
Total unique titles: 8,790

Latest release year: 2021

KEY FINDINGS
The highest yearly title count occurred in 2018, with 1,146 titles.
The CSV includes yearly counts, changes in counts, and percentage changes compared with the previous year.

INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
Release year indicates when a title was originally released, not necessarily when it was added to Netflix.
The dataset is a snapshot of Netflix titles and does not represent every title released worldwide in each year.
The newest years may be incomplete in this dataset, so their lower counts should be interpreted cautiously.
Percentage change is left blank when the previous year has zero titles because the calculation is undefined.
