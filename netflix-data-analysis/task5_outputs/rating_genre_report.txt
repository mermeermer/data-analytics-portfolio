METHODOLOGY AND LIMITATIONS
Genre counts include one association per title and genre. A title can belong to multiple genres.
Rating counts describe the dataset's recorded rating labels. They do not directly measure audience age or viewing behavior.
The rating-by-type chart compares the number of titles. Use the percentage summary for comparisons that account for different totals of Movies and TV Shows.
These results describe the available dataset and do not establish audience preferences or viewing performance.
