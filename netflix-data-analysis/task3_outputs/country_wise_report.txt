METHODOLOGY AND LIMITATIONS
Titles listing multiple countries are counted once for each listed country.
Each title-country combination is counted only once.
Country counts therefore represent title-country associations and may add up to more than the total number of unique titles.
Country percentages use the number of unique titles with at least one known country as the denominator. They may add up to more than 100% because titles can be associated with multiple countries.
These results describe the dataset and do not, by themselves, establish audience preferences or viewing performance.
